ilinblog.ru was a Russian-language educational platform I created in 2017 to publish accessible explanations, project notes, and early research work. I built the original PHP and MySQL website myself and redesigned it several times as I learned web development. I also planned to open the platform to other contributors: readers could introduce themselves and propose topics, after which I could create an author account for them.

## History

The website documented my early work across physics, mathematics, programming, electronics, and scientific research. Its articles ranged from physical simulations and neural networks to digital logic, lasers, game development, and International Young Physicists' Tournament problems.

The original publishing platform is no longer active. I restored it as an independent static archive on its historical domain so the material remains available in context. The archive preserves 18 public articles from 2017–2020, including their original dates, categories, illustrations, videos, formulas, code, and historical view totals. It also contains seven early research works from 2016–2021.

## Restored archive

The restored version no longer uses PHP, a database, accounts, comments, or a live view counter. The original Russian-language articles have not been rewritten or modernized; consequently, some information may now be outdated and a few external embeds are no longer available.
